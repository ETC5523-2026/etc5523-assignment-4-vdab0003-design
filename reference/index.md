## Datasets

Hospital-acquired infection data for Germany and Europe, 2011-12.

- [`hai_burden`](https://etc5523-2026.github.io/etc5523-assignment-4-vdab0003-design/reference/hai_burden.md)

  DALYs
- [`hai_rates`](https://etc5523-2026.github.io/etc5523-assignment-4-vdab0003-design/reference/hai_rates.md)
  : Infections per 100 hospital patients
- [`hai_counts`](https://etc5523-2026.github.io/etc5523-assignment-4-vdab0003-design/reference/hai_counts.md)
